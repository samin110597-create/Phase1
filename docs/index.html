Price-target method

Central target = current price × [1 + (P(up) − P(down)) × daily ATR% × √horizon]. The displayed range is ± one horizon-scaled daily ATR. These are quantitative model-implied targets, not guaranteed future prices.
Historical validation and forward validation are deliberately separate. A forecast remains visible even when historical validation fails; the page tells you the failure instead of replacing it with “pending.”
